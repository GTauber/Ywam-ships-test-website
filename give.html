How often?
Monthly amount

One patient

Every month, $33 helps bring healing in Jesus' name to someone on a remote island who is suffering from a treatable medical problem.

Two patients

Every month, $66 helps two patients in desperate need, on islands where care is hard to reach.

Three people

Every month, $99 provides life-changing treatment, such as cataract surgery and dental extractions, for three people who may never get care any other way.

Everyone on board pays their own way.

Every member of our staff and crew, from the captain to the cooks, raises their own support from the people who send them.

Volunteers go a step further and pay to serve. Their outreach fee covers their own food and accommodation and helps with the ship's upkeep.

So your donation goes to the mission itself: reaching people on islands with no road, no bridge and no runway.

  • 100% of our staff are volunteers
  • Free medical care, in clinics ashore and surgery on board
  • By invitation we go where local governments ask us, and build long-term partnerships

Give to something specific

Name a purpose and your gift is used for it, unless you give us permission to redirect it. Gifts without a purpose go where the need is greatest.

  • The m/v YWAM Liberty

    For 29 years she was L'Astrolabe, resupplying a French research station in Antarctica. Now the shipping containers on her deck become an eye-surgery theatre, a dental clinic, a TB lab and a pharmacy. Help keep her serving from her home port of Madang.

    Give to the Liberty

  • Medical outreach

    Free care where there is none: dental clinics, primary health care, optometry, immunizations, cataract surgery and hygiene education. We pair your gift with the outreach where it fits.

    Give to medical outreach

  • The Flotilla

    Started in 2020, the Flotilla works with private yacht owners to carry mission teams to isolated islands with health care, clean water, education, Bible translation and the Gospel.

    Give to the Flotilla

  • A missionary you know

    Missionary support can only go to people who are actively serving as international missionaries and hold a valid religious visa. Please check they qualify before you give.

Accountable for every gift

You'll see the name YWAM Ships Orange County below and on PayPal. It's our legal name: we began in Orange County, California, in 2011 and became YWAM Ships Kona in 2013, the year Port YWAM opened in Kona. Same organization, same EIN.

Our commitment to you, the donor

YWAM Ships Orange County is committed to using your gifts wisely and efficiently to fulfill our mission. We deeply value your trust and confidence. No goods or services were provided in exchange for this contribution. All donations are tax-deductible and will be used to support YWAM Ships Orange County programs and projects, including—but not limited to—property development, staff support, and outreach efforts.

While we strive to honor donor preferences whenever possible, all gifts are received as unrestricted contributions, allowing us to allocate funds where they are most needed. If a specific initiative is fully funded, or if program needs change, the Board of Directors reserves the right to reallocate funds to the area of greatest impact while ensuring they remain within the scope of our charitable purpose.

Donations designated for a specific purpose will be used accordingly, unless the donor provides permission to redirect them. We provide financial accountability through accurate acknowledgments, annual reports, and independent audits. To request a copy of our audited financial statements, please contact us directly. Our EIN number is 45-3452689.
