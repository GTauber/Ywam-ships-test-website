Meet the Liberty.

  • 65.5 m long
  • 1986 built in Scotland
  • 14 kn top speed
  • 1A Super ice class

For 29 years she was L'Astrolabe, resupplying a French research station in Antarctica. In 2017 her owners sold her to YWAM for $300,000, a fraction of her value. Today the old polar ship carries clinics in shipping containers to the tropics. Tap a part of the ship to see who works there.

The bridge

Watchkeeping, navigation, and anchoring close enough to shore for the Zodiacs to run. About a third of the crew are Papua New Guineans, many of them trained at PNG Maritime College in Madang.

  • Master II/2
  • Chief mate II/2
  • Able seafarer deck II/5
  • Deckhand
Marine roles

The clinic deck

The Liberty is the only ship in the YWAM fleet that carries shipping containers. On deck they become an eye-surgery theatre, a dental clinic, a TB lab and a pharmacy. Primary care and optometry clinics run ashore, and Zodiacs bring surgery patients out to the ship.

  • Doctor
  • Nurse
  • Midwife
  • Pharmacist

  • Dental assistant

The engine room

Two Mirrlees Blackstone diesels, 4,600 kW between them, rebuilt to the crankshaft during the pandemic and back at sea since 2024. Engineers keep her running; tradespeople keep everything else working.
