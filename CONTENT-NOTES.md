# Content notes for the YWAM Ships Kona team

Things to confirm or decide before this site goes live. Everything on the new site comes from the current website, the annual reports, the blog, or press coverage. Where sources disagree, the site uses the value noted here.

## Please confirm

1. **"936 Pacific islands have no airport."** The homepage uses 936, the figure on the current Port YWAM page and on ywam.org. Other pages say 934 inhabited islands, 900+, or 1,000 islands (WaveMakers).
2. **Impact numbers are old.** The homepage shows 654 eyesights restored, 296 villages and 10,995 Bibles, all from 2016–2018, labelled with their years. The only recent figure found is "more than 20,000 medical services in 2025" (EMTV, April 2026, quoting Kristy Wilke). Newer totals for 2025–2026 would make this section much stronger.
3. **2026 status of the Liberty.** EMTV (April 2026) reported outreach voyages paused while the ship supports health services in Madang, "until at least June 2026". Nothing newer was found, and the blog has no posts after September 2025. Confirm what is true now.
4. **Upcoming school dates and prices** come from the current Train pages (January, April, July and September 2027 starts; $10,100, or $11,100 for Ring of Fire and Aloha). Some FAQ pages list older prices.
5. **The Liberty's specs**: 65.5 m, 1,753 GT, built 1986 by Ferguson-Ailsa in Scotland, 14 knots, ice class 1A Super, sold to YWAM in 2017 for $300,000 (valued at $11.6M). These come from the blog, the annual reports and Wikipedia.
6. **Voyage map route.** The line drawn from Kona to Majuro to Madang is illustrative, an island-hopping path, not a track the ship actually sailed. The distances shown (2,044 and 3,746 nautical miles) are great-circle distances from Kona.
7. **Partner quotes** (Loren Cunningham, Governor Sasindran Muthuvel, Dr. Waimbe Wahamu, Christopher J. Loeak) are taken word for word from the current homepage. Dr. Wahamu's is shortened with ellipses.

8. **Photo with the Pacific Link.** `kids-beach-ship-png.jpg` (children dancing on a black-sand beach, used as the About and Stories hero) shows the **Pacific Link**, not the Liberty; the name is on her stern. The alt text now says so. Swap it for a Liberty photo if you prefer.
9. **`about/liberty-story-2025.jpg`** is captioned "The Liberty's crew, 2025". The ship is the Liberty; please confirm the year.
10. **Consent.** The stories show real names and faces, including Jeff Wilke in a hospital bed. Please confirm consent.
