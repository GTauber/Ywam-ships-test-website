Rai Coast, Papua New Guinea

It takes so little to make such a big difference

Early in 2025, on the Liberty's first medical outreach, Susan and the optical team headed for the Rai Coast. No roads reach these villages, so the team switched to smaller boats for a trip that often took several hours.

Some people walked three days from Upper Namkina to get there, said Dr. Claudia Choy, the medical outreach director.
